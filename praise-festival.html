How we got here

The story so far

Three editions in three years, growing from a local gathering into the Pacific Northwest's biggest worship night.

2024

Seattle, Washington

The first edition of Praise Festival — the gathering that started it all, bringing the Pacific Northwest's African and diaspora community together for a single night of worship.

2025

Seattle, Washington

Praise Festival returned to Seattle for a second night, drawing an even bigger crowd and setting up the momentum that carried into the festival's biggest year yet.

2026

Federal Way, Washington

Sold out

Headlined by Sinach at Family Life Center, this edition sold out — the clearest sign yet that Praise Festival has become an anchor event for Africans and the diaspora across the Pacific Northwest and beyond.
