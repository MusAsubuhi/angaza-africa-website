Praise Festival 2026 · Halloween Edition

One stage, one continent, one family.

Praise Festival has grown from a single Seattle gathering into a movement that has filled venues in three countries — and sold out its most recent Washington edition. This Halloween, it comes to ShoWare Center in Kent.

How we got here

The story so far

Three editions in three years, growing from a local gathering to an international festival.

2024

Seattle, Washington

The first edition of Praise Festival — the gathering that started it all, bringing the Pacific Northwest's African and diaspora community together for a single night of worship.

2025

Nairobi, Kenya

Praise Festival crossed the Atlantic for the first time, bringing the movement home to Africa with a festival in Nairobi that connected the diaspora directly to the continent.

2026

Kent / Federal Way, Washington

Sold out

Headlined by Sinach, this edition sold out — the clearest sign yet that Praise Festival has become an anchor event for Africans and the diaspora across the Pacific Northwest and beyond.
