A global movement, born from home

Uniting Africa and her diaspora — in worship, in service, in hope.

Angaza Africa is a faith-driven nonprofit connecting Africans and the diaspora across the globe through worship, humanitarian outreach, and community transformation — from Seattle to Kigali to Nairobi.

See Praise Festival 2026 Our Humanitarian Work

Who we are

Registered in Washington State. Rooted in Rwanda, DRC, and Kenya. Built for the whole diaspora.

Angaza — "to shine light" in Swahili — began with a simple conviction: that Africans scattered across the world and the communities they left behind are still one family. We gather that family through worship events, we serve it through humanitarian work on the ground in Rwanda and the DRC, and we call it home through evangelism and outreach that puts salvation first.

What we do

Three pillars, one mission

Angaza Music

Worship events and artist development that bring African and diaspora voices onto the same stage — anchored by our flagship gathering, Praise Festival.

Angaza Foundation
